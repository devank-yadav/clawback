# Supply and Promotion Agreement 2026: Birchbank Kitchen and Greenfold Natural Distributors

**Agreement no.:** GN-V-20614
**Effective date:** January 1, 2026

**Between:**
- **Birchbank Kitchen** ("Supplier"), Oakland, California, represented by Lucy Tran, Owner (lucy@birchbankkitchen.example)
- **Greenfold Natural Distributors, Inc.** ("Distributor"), Deductions desk: deductions@greenfoldnatural.example

## 1. Purpose and term

1.1 This Agreement sets the prices, frozen delivery terms, deductions and promotions that apply when Distributor
buys Products from Supplier for resale to natural and independent grocery stores.
1.2 The term runs from January 1, 2026 to December 31, 2026.

## 2. Definitions

2.1 "Products" means the Birchbank frozen dumplings and potstickers listed in Schedule A.
2.2 "Case" means one shipping case of eight 12 oz bags.
2.3 "Unit" means one bag sold to an end customer, as reported in Distributor's store sales data.
2.4 "Delivery receipt" means the receipt signed by Distributor's receiver when a frozen delivery is unloaded.

## 3. Prices, invoices and payment

3.1 Distributor will pay the case prices in Schedule A for all deliveries during the term.

3.2 Supplier invoices once a month for all Products delivered in that month, at the case price on each delivery.

3.3 Payment terms are net 21 days from the invoice date.

3.4 A pricing deduction is limited to the difference between the invoiced case price and the Schedule A case price, for the cases on that delivery.

## 4. Frozen deliveries and shortages

4.1 Supplier delivers to Distributor's distribution center at 0 degrees Fahrenheit or colder. Distributor's receiver checks the
temperature log, counts the cases and signs the delivery receipt.

4.2 Shortages are credited only when the receiver notes them on the signed delivery receipt.

4.3 Distributor will submit shortage claims within 10 days of delivery, at the invoiced case price.

4.4 Product refused for temperature is returned on the same truck and is not invoiced.

## 5. March In-Store Savings (promotion GN-MAR-26)

5.1 Supplier will fund a promotional allowance of $0.75 per unit on Birchbank Pork & Chive Dumplings and Birchbank Vegetable Potstickers sold between March 8 and March 28, 2026.

5.2 The promotional allowance applies to a maximum of 4,000 units in total.

5.3 Distributor will recover the allowance by deduction, with a remittance advice that lists the reference, units,
rate and weeks for each deduction, supported by store sales data for the weeks claimed.

## 6. Unsaleables

6.1 Supplier will credit Distributor an unsaleables allowance of up to 1.5% of the invoice value of Products shipped in each calendar month.

6.2 Distributor will deduct unsaleables at actual, up to the monthly allowance, with a store-level unsaleables report.

6.3 Unsaleable Products are destroyed at the store and are not returned to Supplier.

## 7. Fees

7.1 No slotting, new-item or freezer placement fees apply to the Products under this Agreement.
7.2 Any fee not listed in this Agreement requires Supplier's written approval before it is deducted.

## 8. Deductions and disputes

8.1 Each deduction must show its reference, its deduction code and the documents that support it.
8.2 A unit, case or fee is claimed only once; an amount already deducted or credited is not deducted again.

8.3 Supplier will dispute a deduction by written notice to deductions@greenfoldnatural.example within 90 days of the remittance date.

## 9. Changes to this Agreement

9.1 This Agreement changes only in writing: by an amendment signed by both parties, or by a written approval
from Supplier's approved representative, Lucy Tran, Owner (lucy@birchbankkitchen.example).
9.2 No email, call or other statement by either party's staff changes this Agreement unless Section 9.1 is met.

## 10. General

10.1 This Agreement is governed by the laws of the State of California.
10.2 This Agreement, with Schedule A, is the entire agreement between the parties about its subject.
