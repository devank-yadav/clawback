# Supplier Trade Agreement 2026: Fernwell Sparkling Tea Co. and Tidemark Beverage

**Agreement no.:** TM-FW-2026
**Effective date:** February 1, 2026

**Between:**
- **Fernwell Sparkling Tea Co.** ("Supplier"), Portland, Oregon, represented by Priya Raman, Owner (priya@fernwelltea.example)
- **Tidemark Beverage Distributors, LLC** ("Distributor"), Deductions desk: deductions@tidemarkbev.example

## 1. Purpose and term

1.1 This Agreement sets the prices, delivery terms, deductions and promotions that apply when Distributor buys
Products from Supplier for resale to its retail customers.
1.2 The term runs from February 1, 2026 to January 31, 2027.

## 2. Definitions

2.1 "Products" means the Fernwell sparkling teas listed in Schedule A.
2.2 "Case" means one shipping case of six four-packs of 12 oz cans.
2.3 "Unit" means one four-pack sold to an end customer, as reported in Distributor's store scan data.
2.4 "Proof of delivery" means the delivery receipt signed by Distributor's receiver at the distribution center dock.

## 3. Prices, invoices and payment

3.1 Distributor will pay the case prices in Schedule A. These prices replace Supplier's 2025 price list from
February 1, 2026.

3.2 Supplier invoices once a month for all Products delivered in that month, at the case price on each delivery.

3.3 Payment terms are net 30 days from the invoice date.

3.4 Supplier will give Distributor at least 30 days' written notice of any change to the case prices in Schedule A.

3.5 A pricing deduction is limited to the difference between the invoiced case price and the Schedule A case price, for the cases on that delivery.

## 4. Deliveries and shortages

4.1 Distributor's receiver will count each delivery at the dock and sign the proof of delivery, noting any
shortage or damage on it before the driver leaves.

4.2 Shortages are credited only when the shortage is noted on the proof of delivery signed by Distributor's receiver.

4.3 Distributor will submit shortage claims within 15 days of delivery.

4.4 Shortages are credited at the invoiced case price for the cases noted short.

## 5. Spring Feature 2026 (promotion TM-SPR-26)

5.1 Supplier will fund a scan allowance of $1.25 per unit on Fernwell Peach Black Tea, Fernwell Yuzu Green Tea and Fernwell Hibiscus Ginger sold between March 1 and March 28, 2026.

5.2 The scan allowance applies to a maximum of 10,000 units in total.

5.3 The scan allowance is paid on units sold to end customers as shown in Distributor's store scan data, not on cases shipped.

5.4 Fernwell Lemon Mate is not part of the Spring Feature.

5.5 Distributor will recover the scan allowance by deduction, with a remittance advice that lists the reference,
units, rate and weeks for each deduction, and will send the matching store scan data on request.

## 6. Unsaleables

6.1 Supplier will credit Distributor an unsaleables allowance of 2% of the invoice value of Products shipped in each calendar month.

6.2 The unsaleables allowance covers all spoiled, damaged, dented and out-of-date Products, and Supplier does not accept separate claims for unsaleables at actual.

6.3 Distributor will destroy unsaleable Products in the field and will not return them to Supplier.

## 7. Fees

7.1 No slotting, setup or placement fees apply to the Products under this Agreement.
7.2 Any fee not listed in this Agreement requires Supplier's written approval before it is deducted.

## 8. Deductions and disputes

8.1 Each deduction must show its reference, its deduction code and the documents that support it.

8.2 A unit, case or fee is claimed only once; an amount already deducted or credited is not deducted again.

8.3 Supplier will dispute a deduction by written notice to deductions@tidemarkbev.example within 60 days of the remittance date.

8.4 Distributor will answer each dispute within 30 days and repay any amount it cannot support with the documents
named in this Agreement.

## 9. Changes to this Agreement

9.1 This Agreement changes only in writing: by an amendment signed by both parties, or by a written approval
from one of Supplier's approved representatives named in Section 9.2.
9.2 Supplier's approved representatives for changes are Priya Raman, Owner (priya@fernwelltea.example), and
Lena Brooks, Supplier's broker (lena@brookssales.example).
9.3 No email, call or other statement by either party's staff changes this Agreement unless Section 9.1 is met.

## 10. General

10.1 This Agreement is governed by the laws of the State of Oregon.
10.2 This Agreement, with Schedule A, is the entire agreement between the parties about its subject.
