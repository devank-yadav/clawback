# Vendor Agreement 2026: Ridgefire Salsa Co. and Corbel Grocery Supply

**Agreement no.:** CGS-VA-3381
**Effective date:** January 1, 2026

**Between:**
- **Ridgefire Salsa Co.** ("Supplier"), Austin, Texas, represented by Luis Arroyo, Owner (luis@ridgefiresalsa.example)
- **Corbel Grocery Supply Co.** ("Distributor"), Deductions desk: deductions@corbelgrocery.example

## 1. Purpose and term

1.1 This Agreement sets the prices, delivery terms, deductions and promotions that apply when Distributor buys
Products from Supplier for resale to its member grocery stores.
1.2 The term runs from January 1, 2026 to December 31, 2026.

## 2. Definitions

2.1 "Products" means the Ridgefire salsas listed in Schedule A.
2.2 "Case" means one shipping case of twelve 16 oz jars.
2.3 "Unit" means one jar sold to an end customer, as reported in Distributor's store movement data.
2.4 "Feature" means the Spring Ad Feature in Section 5.

## 3. Prices, invoices and payment

3.1 Distributor will pay the case prices in Schedule A for all deliveries during the term.

3.2 Supplier invoices once a month for all Products delivered in that month, at the case price on each delivery.

3.3 Payment terms are net 30 days from the invoice date.

3.4 A pricing deduction is limited to the difference between the invoiced case price and the Schedule A case
price, for the cases on that delivery.

## 4. Deliveries and shortages

4.1 Distributor's receiver will count each delivery at the dock and sign the proof of delivery.

4.2 Shortages are credited only when the receiver notes them on the signed proof of delivery.

4.3 Distributor will submit shortage claims within 15 days of delivery.

## 5. Spring Ad Feature (promotion CG-SPR-26)

5.1 Supplier will fund a promotional allowance of $0.50 per unit on Ridgefire Roasted Tomato Salsa, Ridgefire Salsa Verde and Ridgefire Chipotle Salsa sold between March 1 and March 21, 2026.

5.2 The promotional allowance applies to a maximum of 6,000 units in total.

5.3 Units sold before or after these dates are not covered unless an amendment signed by both parties, or a written approval from one of Supplier's approved representatives, extends the feature.

5.4 Distributor will recover the allowance by deduction, with a remittance advice that lists the reference, units,
rate and weeks for each deduction, supported by store movement data for the weeks claimed.

## 6. Placement and fees

6.1 Distributor will place the Products in the Spring circular for the weeks of the Feature at no charge.
6.2 Any fee not listed in this Agreement requires Supplier's written approval before it is deducted.

## 7. Unsaleables

7.1 Distributor will report unsaleables by store and by item each month.

7.2 Supplier will credit Distributor for unsaleable Products at 2% of monthly purchases or at actual documented unsaleables, as applicable.

7.3 Unsaleable Products are destroyed at the store and are not returned to Supplier.

## 8. Deductions and disputes

8.1 Each deduction must show its reference, its deduction code and the documents that support it.

8.2 A unit, case or fee is claimed only once; an amount already deducted or credited is not deducted again.

8.3 Supplier will dispute a deduction by written notice to deductions@corbelgrocery.example within 60 days of the remittance date.

8.4 Distributor will answer each dispute within 30 days.

## 9. Changes to this Agreement

9.1 This Agreement changes only in writing: by an amendment signed by both parties, or by a written approval
from one of Supplier's approved representatives named in Section 9.2.
9.2 Supplier's approved representatives for changes are Luis Arroyo, Owner (luis@ridgefiresalsa.example), and
Tom Becker, Supplier's broker (tom@beckerfoodsales.example).
9.3 No email, call or other statement by either party's staff changes this Agreement unless Section 9.1 is met.

## 10. General

10.1 This Agreement is governed by the laws of the State of Texas.
10.2 This Agreement, with Schedule A, is the entire agreement between the parties about its subject.
