# Spring Chocolate Promotion 2026: Cocoa Ridge Chocolate Co. and Brightway Grocers

**Agreement ID:** CR-SPRING-26
**Effective date:** February 20, 2026

**Between:**
- **Cocoa Ridge Chocolate Co.** ("Supplier"), represented by Ava Brooks, Owner (ava@cocoaridge.example)
- **Brightway Grocers** ("Distributor"), Deductions desk: deductions@brightwaygrocers.example

## 1. The promotion

1.1 Brightway runs an in-store spring promotion on Cocoa Ridge chocolate bars. Supplier pays Brightway an allowance for each bar sold on promotion.

## 2. Allowance

2.1 Supplier will fund a promotional allowance of $1.00 per bar on Cocoa Ridge Dark Bar and Cocoa Ridge Milk Bar sold between March 1 and March 28, 2026.

2.2 The promotional allowance applies to a maximum of 1,000 units in total. A unit is one bar sold to a shopper.

2.3 Brightway may take the allowance as a deduction from Supplier's invoice, listing the bars, rate and dates for each deduction.

## 3. Display fee

3.1 Brightway may charge a display fee of up to $500 per month at its discretion, with written notice to Supplier.

## 4. Changes

4.1 This Agreement can be changed only by a written amendment signed by both parties.

**Signed:** Ava Brooks, Cocoa Ridge Chocolate Co. · Jordan Price, Brightway Grocers
