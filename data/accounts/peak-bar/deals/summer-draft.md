# DRAFT: Promotional Allowance Agreement: Summer Promo 2026 (SUMMER-26)

> FICTIONAL. Peak Bar Co. and Northmart are invented for the Clawback demo. Not a real contract.
> DRAFT for review. Not signed.

**Agreement ID:** SUMMER-26
**Proposed effective date:** April 20, 2026

**Between:**
- **Peak Bar Co.** ("Supplier"), represented by Dana Okafor, Owner (dana@peakbar.example)
- **Northmart** ("Distributor"), Deductions desk: deductions@northmart.example

## 1. Purpose

Supplier and Distributor agree to run a summer in-store promotion on selected Peak Bar products.

## 2. Definitions

2.1 "Promoted Products" means the products named in Section 3.1.
2.2 "Promotion Period" means the dates named in Section 3.1.
2.3 "Units" means individual retail units sold to end customers, as reported in Distributor's store sales data.

## 3. Promotional allowance

3.1 Supplier will fund a promotional allowance of $1.00 per unit on Peak Bar Original and Peak Bar Chocolate sold between May 1 and May 31, 2026.

3.2 The promotional allowance applies to a maximum of 6,000 units in total.

3.3 Distributor may recover the allowance by deduction from Supplier's invoices, with a remittance advice that
lists the reference, units, rate and period for each deduction.

## 4. Claims and documentation

4.1 Each deduction must be supported by store sales data for the period claimed.
4.2 A unit may be claimed only once. Deductions already paid or credited may not be taken again.
4.3 Supplier may dispute any deduction within 60 days of the remittance date by written notice to
deductions@northmart.example.

## 5. Fees

5.1 No slotting fees apply to the Promoted Products during the Promotion Period.
5.2 Distributor will not charge spoilage or damage fees on Promoted Products without a signed proof-of-damage report.
5.3 Any fee not listed in this Section 5 requires Supplier's written approval.

5.4 Northmart may charge a display fee of up to $1,500 per month at its discretion.

## 6. Changes to this Agreement

6.1 This Agreement may be changed only in writing: by an amendment signed by both parties, or by a written
approval sent by one of Supplier's approved representatives named in Section 6.2.
6.2 Supplier's approved representatives for changes are Dana Okafor (Owner) and Jordan Lee, Supplier's broker
(jordan@peakbar-broker.example).

## 7. General

7.1 This Agreement is governed by the laws of the State of California.
7.2 This Agreement is the entire agreement between the parties about the Summer Promo 2026.
