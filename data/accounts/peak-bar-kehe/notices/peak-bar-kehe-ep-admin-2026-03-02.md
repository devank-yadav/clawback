# KeHE CONNECT: Vendor Policies & Procedures update

**From:** KeHE CONNECT (vendorportal@kehe.example)
**To:** All vendors
**Notice date:** March 2, 2026
**Section:** 6.5 Extra Performance

Effective March 9, 2026, the Extra Performance administrative charge will be 7% on the total amount of each invoice with a maximum of $250 per invoice.

All other terms of Section 6.5 are unchanged. Questions go to your Vendor Manager.
