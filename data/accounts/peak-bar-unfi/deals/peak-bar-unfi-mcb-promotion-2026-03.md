This deal sheet is Peak Bar Co.'s participation in UNFI's March sales and marketing program under Section 2.A of the
ClearVue Supplier Agreement. It sets the only manufacturer chargeback (MCB) Peak Bar Co. funds in March 2026. The
ClearVue Supplier Agreement governs everything this deal sheet does not state.

## 1. Chargeback

1.1 Supplier will fund a manufacturer chargeback of $1.00 per unit on Peak Bar Original and Peak Bar Chocolate sold between March 1 and March 28, 2026.

1.2 The manufacturer chargeback applies to a maximum of 4,000 units in total.

1.3 A unit is one case sold by UNFI to a retailer, as shown in UNFI's MCB backup for the week. Peak Bar Peanut Butter is not part of this deal.
