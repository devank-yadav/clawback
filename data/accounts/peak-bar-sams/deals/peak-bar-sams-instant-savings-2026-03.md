## 1. Instant Savings

1.1 Supplier will fund an instant savings allowance of $2.00 per unit on Peak Bar Original Club Pack and Peak Bar Chocolate Club Pack sold between March 2 and March 29, 2026.

1.2 The instant savings allowance applies to a maximum of 6,000 units in total.

1.3 A unit is one club pack sold to a member, as shown in Sam's Club's point-of-sale data for the week. Peak Bar Variety Club Pack is not part of this event.
